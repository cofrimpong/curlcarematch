# Grounded Haircare Notes

- Low porosity hair often responds better to lightweight moisture and warmth-assisted application.
- Medium porosity hair is usually flexible and benefits from balancing moisture, hold, and density fit.
- High porosity hair often benefits from richer conditioning support and sealing layers.
- Low density hair can get weighed down more easily by very rich products.
- High density hair may need richer formulas or stronger hold because more product distribution is needed.
- Fragrance-free options may be more comfortable for some users with sensitivity.
- Educational language should use phrases like may support moisture, may help with dryness, or is commonly used for softness.
