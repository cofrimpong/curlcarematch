# Safety Rules For Future Chatbot

- Do not claim products diagnose, cure, or medically treat scalp conditions.
- Do not guarantee hair growth.
- Do not invent ingredients, prices, or product fit reasons that are not in the dataset.
- Cite only grounded content from the local product and guide files.
- For psoriasis, severe irritation, hair loss, or ongoing scalp conditions, advise seeing a licensed dermatologist.
